What this report measures

People no longer only type into a search box. They ask an assistant. This report measures both routes to your website and tells you, in plain terms, where you are being found, where you are being trusted, and what to change first.

AI visibility
Whether assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity mention you, describe you correctly, and recommend you when someone asks a question your business should answer. We test a fixed set of real questions across every engine we measure, then check whether you appear and whether the assistant cites you as its source.
Search visibility
Whether your pages rank in traditional search for the terms your customers use, and whether the technical health, content and authority behind those pages support those rankings.
How to read the scores
Both scores run from 0 to 100 and are built from measured signals, not opinion. A score is only useful next to its direction of travel. Where we have too little data to be confident, we say so rather than filling the gap with a number.
